ScanForge: Private Document Scanner

Frequently Asked Questions

Does ScanForge require an internet connection?

No. All scanning, merging, and compressing happens entirely offline, directly on your device.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. ScanForge never uploads, stores, or has access to your documents. Everything stays in your device's local storage.

How do I merge PDFs?

Open ScanForge → PDF Tools → Merge PDFs → select your files → tap Merge.

How do I scan a document?

Open ScanForge → Scan to PDF → take a photo or choose one from your library → the app converts it to a PDF automatically.

How do I compress a PDF?

Open ScanForge → PDF Tools → Compress PDF → select your file → the app reduces the file size while keeping it readable.

Do I need to create an account?

No. ScanForge does not require sign-in or an account for any feature.

The app isn't working, or a scan failed. What should I do?

Please email us with your device model and iOS version, and we'll help you troubleshoot as quickly as possible.
