ScanForge: Private Document Scanner

Who We Are

ScanForge is built by MatriSani IT Solutions Private Limited, a software development company based in Ahmedabad, India. We build mobile and web applications with a focus on clean design, strong performance, and, wherever possible, genuine respect for user privacy.

Why We Built ScanForge

Most document scanner apps quietly route your files through cloud servers — often to power features you never asked for. We wanted to build something different: a scanner and PDF toolkit that does everything a busy person actually needs, without ever touching a server.

ScanForge scans, merges, and compresses your documents entirely on your device. No account. No uploads. No cloud dependency. Just fast, private tools that work the same whether you're online or not.

What We Believe

  • Your documents are yours. We never see, store, or collect them.
  • Privacy shouldn't be a paid feature. Offline-first is the default, not an upsell.
  • Simple tools, done well. Scan, merge, compress — fast and reliable, without clutter.
